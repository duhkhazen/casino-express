Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy describes how the Casino Express publishing tools (“the app”, including the Google OAuth client “Casino Express Uploader”, the Meta app “Casino Express” and the Casino Express TikTok app) handle data. The app is a private tool operated by the owner of the Casino Express channels to publish the channels' own videos and read their own performance statistics. It is not distributed to the public and does not access data of any other user.

Data the app accesses

Google / YouTube. With the channel owner's consent the app uses youtube.upload (upload the channel's videos and their metadata: title, description, tags, privacy status and scheduled publish time), youtube.readonly (read the channel's own videos, their status and public counters) and yt-analytics.readonly (read the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports, such as views and average view percentage).

Meta (Instagram and Facebook). For the Casino Express Instagram professional account and its linked Facebook Page, the app publishes the channel's own Reels and reads their insights (views, reach, watch time, shares, saves), and updates the Page's own profile picture, cover photo and description.

TikTok. With the account owner's authorization through TikTok Login Kit, the app uses user.info.basic (identify the authorized account), video.upload (send the channel's own videos to the TikTok inbox as drafts), video.publish (post the channel's own videos with their caption) and video.list (read public counters of the account's own videos: views, likes, comments and shares).

The app only handles videos created by Casino Express and statistics about them. It does not collect personal data about viewers, followers or any other users.

How data is stored

Access and refresh tokens issued by Google, Meta and TikTok, and the statistics the app reads, are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. They are never sent to any server other than the platform that issued them.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data goes only to Google/YouTube, Meta and TikTok, as needed to publish the videos and read their statistics.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions; Meta in Facebook Settings → Business integrations; TikTok in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. The locally stored tokens and statistics can be deleted from the computer running the app; to request deletion, write to the contact address below.
