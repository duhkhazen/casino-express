Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy describes how Casino Express Uploader (“the app”) handles data. The app is a private tool operated by the owner of the Casino Express channel to publish the channel's own videos. It is not distributed to the public.

Data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests a single permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos to the signed-in YouTube channel. The app uploads video files and their metadata (title, description, tags, privacy status and scheduled publish time) that the channel owner created.

The app does not read, collect or store any other data from Google accounts, and it does not access data of any other user.

How data is stored

The OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Google are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, so uploads can run without signing in every time. They are never sent to any server other than Google's.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data goes only to Google/YouTube, as needed to upload the videos.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Casino Express Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the locally stored tokens can be deleted from the computer running the app.
